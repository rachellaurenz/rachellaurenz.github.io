Just Imagine It

Animation 1 Final

Just Imagine It

Just Imagine It is my Animation 1 Final. We had to create a movie trailer for an original idea. This idea was a Main Character that was sucked into her notebook. In this new world she found everything she drew in the notebook had come to life, including people. She finds her sister (who she had previously draw) and they explore the world together until they come across something the Main Character did not draw.
